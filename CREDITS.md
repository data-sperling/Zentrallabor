# Computation of Synthetic 1D Proton NMR Data

Sample NMR data was generated using: https://www.nmrdb.org/
Copyright: Luc Patiny

Banfi, D.; Patiny, L. www.nmrdb.org: Resurrecting and Processing NMR Spectra On-line. Chimia, 2008, 62(4), 280–281.

Fast and Accurate Algorithm for the Simulation of NMR Spectra of Large Spin Systems. Andrés M. Castillo, Luc Patiny, and 
Julien Wist. Journal of Magnetic Resonance, 2011. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2010.12.008

Aires-de-Sousa, M.; Hemmer, J.; Gasteiger, J. "Prediction of 1H NMR Chemical Shifts Using Neural Networks.
" Analytical Chemistry, 2002, 74(1), 80–90.

Most of the proton descriptors are explained in this work. They were used for the prediction of 1H NMR chemical shifts 
using counterpropagation neural networks.

Y. Binev, J. Aires-de-Sousa, "Structure-Based Predictions of 1H NMR Chemical Shifts Using Feed-Forward Neural Networks."
